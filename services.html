Interior Painting

Interior painting in Madison & Fitchburg

Whether it's one room or the whole house, interior painting is where prep and protection make the difference. We cover floors and furniture, fix the small flaws and leave clean, straight lines.

How it works: Estimate → color and sheen plan → protect and prep → paint → walkthrough.

Get a interior painting estimateSee before & afters

What's included

  • Walls, ceilings, trim, baseboards and doors
  • Moving and covering furniture, floors and fixtures
  • Patching nail holes and small cracks, sanding and caulking
  • Priming where needed, then finish coats
  • Daily clean-up and a final walkthrough

Ideal for

  • Refreshing dated colors before or after a move
  • Accent walls, nurseries and home offices
  • Winter projects while exterior season is closed
Exterior Painting

Exterior house painting for Wisconsin weather

Wisconsin's freeze-thaw cycles are hard on paint. A lasting exterior job comes down to prep: cleaning, scraping loose paint, sealing gaps and priming bare wood before the finish coats go on.

How it works: Estimate → schedule inside the May–October season → prep → paint → walkthrough.

Get a exterior painting estimateSee before & afters

What's included

  • Siding, trim, soffits, fascia, doors and shutters
  • Cleaning and surface prep before painting
  • Scraping and sanding loose or failing paint
  • Caulking gaps and priming bare areas
  • Protecting landscaping, walkways and windows

Ideal for

  • Peeling, chalky or faded siding
  • Boosting curb appeal before listing a home
  • Changing your home's color scheme
Cabinet Refinishing

Kitchen cabinet refinishing

Painting or refinishing cabinets can transform a kitchen or bath at a fraction of the disruption of a remodel. It's detail work — careful cleaning, sanding and priming are what make the finish hold up to daily use.

How it works: Estimate → color and finish choice → remove and prep → finish → reinstall and walkthrough.

Get a cabinet refinishing estimateSee before & afters

What's included

  • Cabinet boxes, doors and drawer fronts
  • Removing and labeling doors and hardware
  • Degreasing, sanding and priming
  • Finish coats suited to high-touch surfaces
  • Reinstalling doors and hardware

Ideal for

  • Oak or dated cabinets in solid condition
  • Kitchen and bathroom updates on a budget
  • Pairing with an interior repaint
Deck Staining

Deck staining and sealing

Sun, snow and foot traffic wear decks down fast. Cleaning, prep and a quality stain restore color and help protect the wood from moisture.

How it works: Estimate → schedule dry-weather window → clean and prep → stain → walkthrough.

Get a deck staining estimateSee before & afters

What's included

  • Deck boards, railings and stairs
  • Cleaning and surface prep
  • Light sanding where needed
  • Stain or sealer application
  • Protecting nearby siding and plants

Ideal for

  • Grey, weathered or blotchy decks
  • Refreshing a deck before summer
  • Newer decks ready for first stain
Drywall Repair

Drywall repair before painting

Fresh paint only looks as good as the wall under it. We repair damage and match texture so patches disappear once the room is painted.

How it works: Estimate → repair → sand and prime → paint → walkthrough.

Get a drywall repair estimateSee before & afters

What's included

  • Nail pops, dents, holes and cracks
  • Patching larger damaged sections
  • Taping, mudding and sanding
  • Texture matching where possible
  • Priming and painting the repair

Ideal for

  • Moving in or out
  • Walls with years of wear before a repaint
  • Repairs after plumbing or electrical work
Our process

Five steps from estimate to walkthrough

  1. Free estimate

    Call or send photos. We review the project and give you an estimate with scope and timing.

  2. Plan & colors

    We confirm surfaces, colors, sheens and a start date that works for you.

  3. Prep & protect

    Covering floors, furniture and landscaping, then patching, sanding, caulking and priming.

  4. Paint

    Even, clean coats and sharp lines, with the work area tidied at the end of each day.

  5. Walkthrough

    We walk the finished job with you and take care of any touch-ups before we call it done.
