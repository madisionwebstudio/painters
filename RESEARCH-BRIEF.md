### 5 things Roll n' Brush will do better
1. **Proof on every page** — drag-to-compare before/after sliders with town + scope captions. Competitors show empty galleries, map images or no photos.
2. **Only local, verbatim reviews** (first name + last initial + town). No mixed-market testimonials.
3. **Fastest path to a quote** — sticky call + estimate bar on mobile and a short form with **photo upload** on every page (no scheduling app, no video required).
4. **Cleaner technical SEO** — one clean URL per page, NAP identical to GBP, tel links that match, ≤60-char titles, HousePainter + FAQ schema, static site built for 90+ Lighthouse vs heavy Squarespace/Webflow/franchise WordPress.
5. **Fitchburg-first + honest seasonality** — hometown in H1/NAP, month-aware messaging (exterior window May–Oct, winter interior), and a plain 5-step process.

## 3. Design direction

**Concept: "Paint chip" — warm, crafted, local.** Not the blue-and-orange contractor template.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Spruce (primary) | `#1E3A34` | Header, headings, footer |
| Linen (background) | `#F7F3EC` | Page background |
| Clay (CTA) | `#A8481F` | Buttons, links on light bg (white text passes AA) |
| Butter (accent) | `#F2C14E` | Stars, small highlights on dark bg |
| Ink (body text) | `#22292B` | Body copy |
| Stone (lines) | `#DDD5C8` | Borders, dividers |

**Fonts:** Fraunces (display serif — painterly, premium) + Public Sans (clean, very readable, civic/local feel). Two families, limited weights, `display=swap`.

**Visual motifs:** paint-swatch cards for services, a brushed underline under the H1 keyword, generous whitespace, real project photos as the hero (placeholder until supplied).

**Seasonality (Wisconsin):** exterior season ≈ May–October, interior year-round. The site swaps its top banner by month automatically:
- May–Aug: "Exterior season is filling up — book your estimate."
- Sep–Oct: "Last weeks of exterior season — reserve fall slots or lock in spring."
- Nov–Apr: "Winter is interior season — [PLACEHOLDER: winter interior offer]. Spring exterior dates are open."
