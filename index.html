FAQ

House painting questions, answered

How much does it cost to paint a house in Wisconsin?

Every home is different, so we price each project individually. Cost depends on the size and height of the home or rooms, how many surfaces are being painted (walls, ceilings, trim, doors, siding), how much prep is needed — scraping, patching, caulking, priming — and the paint you choose. The fastest way to a real number is a free estimate: send a few photos through the form or call (608) 440-4428. [PLACEHOLDER: optional typical price range, only if you want to publish one]

When is the best time to paint a house exterior in Wisconsin?

In south-central Wisconsin, exterior painting season generally runs from about May through October. Paint needs a dry surface and air and surface temperatures above the minimum on the product label — often somewhere between 35°F and 50°F depending on the paint — including overnight while it cures. Late spring and early fall are often ideal thanks to milder temperatures and lower humidity. Exterior calendars fill up quickly, so it pays to book early.

Can you paint the interior of my home in winter?

Yes. Interior painting happens year-round, and winter is a great time to tackle it — exterior season is over, so there's usually more flexibility on scheduling. If you're planning a spring exterior, winter is also the right time to get your estimate and reserve a date. [PLACEHOLDER: winter interior offer, if you run one]

How long does it take to paint a house?

It depends on the scope: a single bedroom, a whole interior and a full exterior are very different jobs, and weather affects exterior timelines.
